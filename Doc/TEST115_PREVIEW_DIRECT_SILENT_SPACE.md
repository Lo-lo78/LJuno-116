# TEST115 — Direct-source preview and silent Space

- Alt+P selects whether Space belongs to REAPER or preview.
- When preview is selected, Space starts/stops the patterns of all three sources without spoken on/off messages.
- Preview can sound Layer 1, Layer 2 and Noise while Note Source remains Direct or LArp.
- Note Source parameters are never changed by preview, and normal MIDI routing resumes automatically on stop.
- The preview stays running if Note Source changes, and the sequencer clock stays active through Pause steps.
- Alt+M still selects Song or Pattern; Alt+P and Alt+M remain spoken.
